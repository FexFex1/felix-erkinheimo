High ticket B2B sales team

Outreach portal inside Attio

Custom Attio interface that filters contacts by segment, drafts the message, and lets sales personalize each one.

Why, what & how

Why

The team sells high-ticket B2B, and mass email was not an option for its target group: every message has to read as personal. Writing each email one by one was too slow, though. They needed to send more outreach, faster, without losing the personal touch, and in the Attio CRM where the team already works.

What

Built a custom UI element in Attio that filters people by market segment and writes a message draft for the segment. The salesperson edits and personalizes each outgoing message. Outreach is faster and every message stays personal.

How

Planned and deployed with Claude Code from the terminal, based on Attio's SDK App documentation.
